About Me

I am a composer for film and drama, creating original music for visual storytelling. My work blends orchestral writing, hybrid textures, and contemporary sound to shape emotion, atmosphere, and narrative.

From tension and action to lyrical and emotional themes, I approach each project with the story in mind— creating music that moves with the picture while standing on its own.

VISION

I believe creativity is a gift entrusted to us, and I want to use what I have been given with purpose, excellence, and gratitude.

I believe music should become part of the story. I craft scores that enhance immersion, shape emotion, and elevate the narrative of each scene.

Ultimately, I hope my music can move people’s hearts, spark imagination, and make a positive and lasting impact beyond the screen.
